GAC Scout+

Scout your Grand Arena opponent in one click on swgoh.gg. Version 2.3.

Counter odds

Your two GAC histories blended with swgoh.gg's counters page, matched to their exact defence squad when there's enough data, and adjusted for your relic levels.

Battle plan

The best combination of your counters against the defences they usually set, never reusing a unit or anything on your defence.

Your defence

Add what you've placed and the plan stops using it. Picking a leader adds your usual team. Saved for rounds 2 and 3.

Defence planner

Squads you own that hold best against the attack teams your opponent uses. Keeps GLs for attack unless you say otherwise.

Zones and banners

Where they usually put each team (Front top, Front bottom, Back, Fleet), and counters ranked by expected banners.

Datacrons

The datacron they last ran on each defence and attack team: level, set and who it boosts. Lvl 12+ gets a warning.

Round by round

Each of their rounds: score, first attack time and delay, what held on their defence and what they dropped.

Their attacks

What they win with and which defences stopped them.

When they attack

Their usual attack hours in your time zone, and average delay.

Roster compare

GP, GLs, relic counts, zetas, omicrons, ships, and every unit side by side.

2. Use it

  1. Open any page on swgoh.gg. If you open your opponent's profile, their ally code fills in and it starts straight away.
  2. Click the GAC Scout+ bookmark. On Android Chrome, type GAC Scout+ in the address bar and tap the bookmark.
  3. The first time, enter your own ally code too. It's remembered in your browser, and you can change it in the header.
  4. Press Scout. The first run takes about 30 to 60 seconds. Later runs are much faster because finished rounds and swgoh.gg counters pages are cached.
  5. Format is Auto (their latest finished event). In the first week of a new season, set it to 3v3 or 5v5 yourself, because swgoh.gg only lists an event once it's over.
  6. Add the units you've put on defence under Your defence so the battle plan doesn't use them. Your fleet defence is filled in from your latest season; fix it there if you've changed it. Press Clear all when a new GAC event starts.

If it doesn't work

  • Nothing happens, or it says it only runs on swgoh.gg: the bookmark has to be clicked while a swgoh.gg page is open in that tab.
  • "swgoh.gg wouldn't give me...": reload the swgoh.gg page, pass its "checking your browser" step, sign in to swgoh.gg if it asks, and click the bookmark again.
  • "Couldn't find a player": check the ally code. Both players need a profile on swgoh.gg.
  • Some pages didn't load: swgoh.gg was busy. Press Scout again after a minute; pages already read are remembered.
  • No history for a new opponent: swgoh.gg may not have their GAC rounds, or they haven't played this format recently. Try the other format.

Where the numbers come from

  • Battles from your GAC history and your opponent's (up to 8 seasons in the same format), plus swgoh.gg's GAC counters page for each of their defences.
  • Est. for you starts from the swgoh.gg win rate, adds the battles in your two histories, and is nudged up to ±12% for the relic gap between your counter and their defence. Each figure is labelled solid, some or thin data.
  • It runs on swgoh.gg itself and reads the same public pages you can open, from your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
  • The bookmark loads the tool's code from this site (GitHub Pages) each time you click it. The source is in the tools/gac-scout folder of the repository.
  • Datacrons are shown (from their newest battles) but kept out of the win %, because sets rotate every few weeks. Mods and omicrons are invisible, so treat the odds as a guide.
